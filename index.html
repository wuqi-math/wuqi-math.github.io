Mathematical Notes

The handshaking lemma

In a finite undirected graph, each edge has two ends. Counting these ends by vertex gives the sum of all vertex degrees. Counting them by edge gives twice the number of edges.

That sum is even. The even degrees contribute an even amount, so the odd degrees must also sum to an even number. Consequently, there are an even number of odd-degree vertices.

Counting walks with an adjacency matrix

Let A be the adjacency matrix of a finite simple graph. The entry at row u, column v of Ak counts the walks of length k from u to v.

For k = 1, this is the definition of A. To get a walk of length k + 1, take a walk of length k and append one edge. The sum in matrix multiplication counts exactly these choices.
